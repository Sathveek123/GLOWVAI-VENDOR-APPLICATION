# 📱 Mobile Screen 23: GST Invoices & TCS/TDS Statements

## 📍 File & Route Specification
- **File Path**: `mobile/app/(vendor)/tax-documents.tsx`
- **Route URL**: `/(vendor)/tax-documents`
- **Access Level**: Authenticated Merchant

---

## 🎯 Purpose & Business Logic
Tax documents center for beauty retailers managing GST commission invoices, sales tax summaries, and Section 52 TCS / Section 194O TDS credit statements. Communicates clear legal boundaries: documents are informational e-commerce business records for GSTR-1 filing, not personalized tax advice.

---

## 🎨 Visual Design & UI System
- **Header & FY Selector**: Title `"Tax Documents"`, masked GSTIN `37AAAAA****1Z5`, Financial Year selector (`FY 2026–27 ▼`), and Legal Disclaimer Banner (`ℹ️ Documents are generated by GlowVAI and your sales records. Please consult your tax advisor for filing.`).
- **Legal Disclaimer Modal**: Tapping `ℹ️ Info` opens full disclaimer modal detailing non-advice boundaries and statutory remittance rules.
- **3 Accessible Tabs**:
  1. **📄 Invoices**: Service fee invoices with period (`October 2026`), taxable value, GST amount, status pills (`AVAILABLE`, `FILED`, `EXPIRED`), and PDF download action.
  2. **📊 GST Summary**: Tax period overview table breaking down taxable sales and GST by slabs: **18% GST Slab** (Cosmetics & Perfumes) and **5% GST Slab** (Essential Skincare & Soaps). Includes `📥 Export GSTR-1` action.
  3. **🏛️ TCS & TDS**: Section 52 TCS (1%) and Section 194O TDS (1%) credit logs with Form 27EQ/26Q reference numbers.

---

## ⚡ Key Features & Data Flow
- **GST Rate Slab Table**: Itemizes CGST, SGST, and Total GST across 18% and 5% slabs for GSTR-1 filing.
- **Expired Download Refresh**: Triggers link re-generation for expired PDF download links.
- **Partial-Year Banner**: Displays `"⚠️ Data for FY 2026-27 is complete up to Q2 (Sep 2026)."`

---

## ♿ Edge & Lifecycle States
- **No Documents**: Renders empty state `"No tax documents match your filter."` with a `"Restore Documents"` CTA.
- **Download Error**: Triggers alert modal `"⚠️ Document Download Failed — Network timeout."`
