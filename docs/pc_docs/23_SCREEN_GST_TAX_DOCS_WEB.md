## 🎯 Purpose & Desktop Interaction System
The GlowVAI Vendor GST & Tax Document Center provides beauty store merchants and charter accountants with official statutory tax invoices, quarterly Section 52 TCS credit statements, Section 194O TDS tax certificates, and itemized GST rate slab summaries for GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B tax filing.

### Key Desktop Workflows:
1. **Mandatory Legal Disclaimer Banner**:
   `"ℹ️ Legal Tax Disclaimer: Documents are generated by GlowVAI and your sales records. Please consult your tax advisor for filing."`
2. **GSTIN Tax Identity Header**: Shows verified state GSTIN (`37AAAAA0000A1Z5 - Verified`), financial year selector (`FY 2026–27`, `FY 2025–26`), and GSTR-1 JSON/Excel batch exporter.
3. **Top Stat Cards**:
   - **Service Fee Invoices**: Taxable `₹6,602.00` + 18% GST (`₹1,188.00`) = `₹7,790.00`
   - **Section 52 TCS Credit**: 1% CGST/SGST (`₹1,665.00`) remitted via Form 27EQ
   - **Section 194O TDS Credit**: 1% IT (`₹1,665.00`) deducted & deposited via Form 26Q
4. **Three Accessible Workspace Tabs**:
   - **📄 Tax Invoices**: Full data table of monthly platform commission and logistics invoices.
   - **📊 GST Rate Slab Breakdown**: Itemized 18% GST slab (Cosmetics & Perfumes) vs 5% GST slab (Essential Skincare & Soaps) CGST/SGST calculations.
   - **🏛️ TCS & TDS Statutory Credit Logs**: Quarterly tax deposit logs for electronic cash ledger claiming.
